Automating RFQ Data Extraction for Pump and Flow Equipment with AI
Automate your entire RFQ-to-quote workflow across document extraction, product matching, pricing retrieval, and ERP quote generation.
001
Parse complex RFQs with precision
The agent ingests emailed PDFs, scanned BOMs, and Excel spec sheets—extracting item descriptions, quantities, part numbers, duty points, materials, and application data with confidence scoring for human review.
002
Intelligent product matching across catalogs and vendor systems
AI automation cross-references extracted specs against your item master and supplier catalogs, differentiating stock items from engineered-to-order equipment and surfacing approved alternates when specified models are obsolete or long-lead.
003
Quote-ready output mapped directly to your ERP
The system retrieves current pricing and lead times, applies customer-specific margins and terms, and generates structured quote lines ready to push into your ERP or CPQ—complete with pump curves, datasheets, and compliance documentation.
How Cassidy automates using AI
Step 1: Trigger on inbound RFQ
The Workflow activates when a new email arrives in your sales inbox or when files land in a monitored SharePoint or Google Drive folder—capturing PDFs, Excel BOMs, and spec attachments automatically.
Step 2: Extract and normalize RFQ data
Cassidy parses each document using OCR and table detection, pulling line items, quantities, part numbers, duty conditions (flow, head, NPSH), materials, seal types, motor specs, and connection requirements into structured fields with confidence scores.
Step 3: Match items to your catalog
The Workflow searches your Knowledge Base and connected item master for exact and fuzzy matches, identifying stock pumps versus engineered-to-order equipment and flagging approved alternates or up-sell opportunities based on duty and materials.
Step 4: Retrieve pricing and lead times
Cassidy pulls current costs from vendor price books, distributor agreements, and supplier portals—applying customer-specific pricing matrices, margin rules, and freight calculations to each line item.
Step 5: Generate quote and supporting documents
The Workflow builds structured quote lines mapped to your ERP fields (SKUs, UOM, tax codes, BOMs) and packages the customer-facing quote with pump curves, datasheets, and compliance statements.
Step 6: Route for review and send
Low-confidence extractions, non-standard materials, or margin exceptions route to your applications engineer for approval. Once approved, Cassidy emails the quote, logs the activity to your CRM, and sets follow-up tasks.
