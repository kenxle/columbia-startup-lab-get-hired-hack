# Product Name Workshop

**What this is:** a working page for picking the product name with Ken. Comment on any option, or answer the questions below. Claude adds options and checks names as we go. The pick goes into the [brand position](BRAND_POSITION.md).

## Questions for you

Answered: the name is decided (see [Decision](#decision)).

- **Q1. "RudCo [Name]" or a name that stands alone?** Under "RudCo," the name can be plain and descriptive. On its own, it needs to carry more.
- **Q2. Describe the job, or carry an image?** "QuoteTrace" says what it does. "Assay" is a metaphor the industry already knows.
- **Q3. Which idea should it lead with?** The research points at three: the paper trail (traceability), accuracy (reading right), or openness (price and source in the open).

## What a good name does here

- **A rep would say it out loud.** "Run it through QuoteTrace." It sounds like a RudCo product line, not a startup.
- **It echoes RudCo's own words.** Their tagline is "Precision. Transparency. Trust." They already sell "PO→COA/MTR" traceability ([RudCo profile](../research/20261010.02_rudco_profile/rudco_profile.md)).
- **It points at our angle:** a quote that shows its work.
- **No obvious clash** with an industrial software product.

## Options, grouped by idea

Conflict checks are quick web searches. Ken's call: this is an internal name for a feature of RudCo's service, not a standalone product, so a name other companies use is fine.

### The paper trail

| Name | Why it fits | Check |
|---|---|---|
| **QuoteTrace** | Every line traces to its source, like a heat number traces to its mill. Current recommendation. | No product found. Closest is QuoTrak, for trade contractors |
| **LineTrace** | Same idea, focused on the line. "Every line shows its work." | No quoting product found. The search turned up only unrelated "line trace" features (IBM, Altair) |
| **Heatline** | Joins the heat number (how metal is traced) to the quote line. Insider-sounding. | No quoting product found |

### Accuracy, borrowed from metalwork

| Name | Why it fits | Check |
|---|---|---|
| **Assay** | An assay tests what a metal really is. The product tests what each RFQ line really means. Short, and true to the trade. | No quoting product found |
| **Caliper** | The tool a metal worker trusts to measure exactly. Points at the gauge trap. | No quoting product found |
| **Gauge** | The demo's star moment is the gauge trap. Also means "to measure." | Common word. Many products use it, so it is hard to own |

### Openness

| Name | Why it fits | Check |
|---|---|---|
| **Open Quote** | Says "price in the open." Echoes "Opacity is a tax." | Taken by an open-source insurance quoting system |
| **ClearQuote** | Says RudCo's transparency message in one word. Ken's favorite, with Quote Desk. | Used by other products; fine as an internal RudCo name |
| **Line Check** | Plain and honest. | Free, but sounds like a checker rather than a quote tool |

### Avoid

- **Quotematic:** an existing AI RFQ-to-quote platform for industrial distributors ([startuphub.ai](https://www.startuphub.ai/startups/quotematic)).
- **Quote Desk:** taken twice (brand position doc).

## Claude's shortlist

1. **RudCo QuoteTrace.** It says the job and the trust idea in one word, and it is open.
2. **RudCo Assay.** It is the strongest metaphor, and a metals buyer gets it instantly. It needs one line of explanation for anyone outside the trade.
3. **RudCo LineTrace.** It is QuoteTrace's sibling, and fits the tagline "Every line shows its work."

## Decision

**RudCo Quote Desk** (Ken, 2026-10-10). It names the rep's desk, where the agent prepares the quote and the rep has the conversation. It is an internal name for part of RudCo's service. Recorded in the [brand position](BRAND_POSITION.md).
