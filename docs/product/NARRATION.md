## Narration

RudCo Quote Desk turns a messy RFQ email, PDF or spreadsheet into a quote the rep can check. It shows the source behind each reading, matches catalog items and carries supported prices. When something is unclear, it asks the buyer instead of guessing. The answer updates the line, and the rep reviews and approves the quote before export.
