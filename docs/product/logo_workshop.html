Q4. Which product mark (P1 to P5)? Claude recommends P1: it is the closest relative of RudCo's mark, it stays clear at 16 px, and the tick says what the product does. Where it goes: the favicon and the Devpost page. RudCo's mark stays in the app header, per your note.

Rules every option keeps

  • RudCo's mark is used as the site serves it. Never redrawn, recolored, stretched, or traced.
  • The mark's height matches the header's 32 px. Words sit centered on it.
  • RudCo's mark is never changed. The Quote Desk mark is a separate drawing that borrows only the bar shape and colors.
  • Type is the system sans: 600 for RudCo, 500 for Quote Desk, per the style guide.
