## Claims we make, and claims we don't

**Price claims.** We never say RudCo is cheapest. Every price explanation says the price tracks published metal cost plus RudCo's margin ([WORKFLOW step 5](WORKFLOW.md#5-price-flag-compliance-list-assumptions)).

**We learn the buyer.** It is how we beat competitors on their own claim. Proton and Canals say they learn customers' shorthand. Ours shows where each learned reading came from: "Read as 0.060 in, as you confirmed on RFQ-0001" ([workflow step 9](WORKFLOW.md#9-learn-the-buyer), [competitive analysis](../research/20261010.03_rudco_competitive_analysis/competitive_analysis.md#where-rudco-can-get-ahead)).

**What we do not claim.** We do not imply live inventory, credit terms, cutting, or ERP links. Lead times follow RudCo's stated promise or say "to confirm". The demo covers quote preparation only ([competitive analysis](../research/20261010.03_rudco_competitive_analysis/competitive_analysis.md#what-our-demo-should-show)).

## Voice and messaging

### RudCo's voice, as it appears

- **Short, declarative lines.** "Industrial materials simplified." and "Precision. Transparency. Trust." ([site code](https://www.rud-co.com/assets/index-B04wKX3n.js)).
- **Names the cost of hiding things.** "Opacity is a tax on industrial procurement." and "The cost doesn’t show up as price. It shows up as friction." ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/www%2Erud-co%2Ecom_opacity-is-a-tax-on-industrial-procurement-activity-7451955371537059840-lsR0)).
- **Speaks to the buyer's doubt.** "The market moves every day. The quote in your inbox often doesn't." ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/www%2Erud-co%2Ecom_have-you-ever-wondered-if-youre-getting-activity-7459610821522833408--F-1)).
- **Keeps people central.** "People still want to deal with people, especially when the decision is important" ([Digital Journal](https://www.digitaljournal.com/article/ai-infrastructure-boom-reshapes-industrial-materials-procurement/)).
- **Plain buttons.** The site's most common button labels are "Request a Quote" and "Contact Us" ([site code](https://www.rud-co.com/assets/index-B04wKX3n.js)).

### Tagline options

Each sits under "Precision. Transparency. Trust."

1. **Every line shows its work.** (Recommended. It is the product in five words, and it answers "Opacity is a tax on industrial procurement.")
2. **Read right. Priced in the open. Sent by your rep.** (One phrase for each word of "Precision. Transparency. Trust.")
3. **A price with its date and its source.** (Answers "The market moves every day. The quote in your inbox often doesn't.")

**Pitch line** (from the [brief](PRD.md#who-it-is-for)): "The agent prepares the conversation, and the rep owns it." It restates the founder's "better information for the conversation" in our terms. Use it to close the video, as [WORKFLOW](WORKFLOW.md#the-video-demo-in-order) says.

### Words to use and avoid

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| quote, line, RFQ, buyer, rep | "AI-powered", "autonomous", "magic" |
| read as, matched to, priced against | "cheapest", "lowest price", "best price" |
| benchmark, dated, published metal cost plus RudCo's margin | "real-time" or "live" pricing (the data is sample data) |
| flag, mill test report (MTR), certificate of analysis (COA), country of origin, DFARS, REACH, RoHS | "certified" or "compliant" (we flag what the buyer needs; we do not certify stock) |
| lead time, ships in 24 to 48 hours (stocked lines), to confirm | "in stock" or a ship date for an item the sample catalog does not mark as stocked |
| ask, question, needs the buyer, the conversation | "replaces your rep", "hands-free" |
| sample data | "instant", "guaranteed" |

### Sample UI microcopy

The SKU, benchmark and prices below are placeholders. The real screen fills them from the sample catalog and labels them as sample data.

- **Ready line:** "Ready. Read as 304 stainless sheet, 0.0625 in (16 ga, stainless gauge table), 48 x 96 in. Matched to SKU [SKU]. Price tracks [benchmark name, date] plus RudCo's margin. Lead time: 24 to 48 hours (stocked line, sample catalog)."
- **Lead time not known:** "Lead time: to confirm. This item is not marked as stocked."
- **Needs-the-buyer line:** "Needs the buyer. Line 3 says 16 gauge and 0.060 in. Those are different thicknesses. Question drafted."
- **Compliance flag:** "Mill test report needed. Triggered by "SA-240" in line 4, an ASME pressure-vessel spec."
- **COA flag:** "Certificate of analysis needed. Triggered by "COA required" in the email body. Applies to lines 2 and 3."
- **REACH / RoHS flag:** "REACH and RoHS declarations requested. Triggered by "REACH/RoHS compliant" in the attachment, page 1."
- **Context flag:** "DFARS origin rules may apply. Triggered by the ship-to address, not the line. Confirm with the buyer."
- **No match:** "No match. Nothing in RudCo's catalog fits this line. Not priced."
- **Buyer question email, subject:** "RFQ-0001, line 3: 16 gauge or 0.060 in?"
- **Buyer question email, opening line:** "Thanks for your RFQ. Lines 1 and 2 are priced. Before we quote line 3, one question: do you need 16 gauge (0.0625 in) or exactly 0.060 in? Click the one you need below."

### Status colors

The site already has status tokens: `--available` (green), `--medium` (amber) and `--unavailable` (gray). We keep those hues and darken the text so each label passes 4.5:1. "No match" uses the site's gray. The site uses gray for unavailable items, and a line RudCo does not carry is that case. Red would read as a fault.

| Status | Text | Tint | Text on tint | Text on white | Based on |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ready | `#126932` | `#e7f9ed` | 6.20:1 | 6.79:1 | `--available: 142 71% 45%` |
| Needs the buyer | `#7f4205` | `#fdf0d8` | 6.94:1 | 7.82:1 | `--medium: 38 92% 50%` |
| No match | `#454e5e` | `#eaecf0` | 7.09:1 | 8.39:1 | `--unavailable: 220 15% 50%` |

Don't use the site's own solid pairs for status badges. White on `--available` is 2.30:1 and white on `--unavailable` is 4.38:1; both fail. Dark text on solid amber (`#1a212d` on `#f59f0a`) passes at 7.58:1 and is fine for a filled "Needs the buyer" badge. Status must also show as a word, never color alone.

### Type, radii, buttons

- **Type:** the system sans stack. The site loads no web fonts (no `@font-face` in the CSS) and uses Tailwind's default `ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif`. Numbers and SKUs can use the site's `font-mono` stack: `ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Menlo, Monaco, Consolas`.
- **Weights:** 500 for nav, 600 for the wordmark and labels, 700 for headings (`font-medium`, `font-semibold`, `font-bold` in the [site code](https://www.rud-co.com/assets/index-B04wKX3n.js)).
- **Radius:** `--radius: .375rem` (6px). Buttons and inputs use `rounded-md`, which is 4px.
- **Buttons:** the site uses the shadcn button set. Default is blue fill with white text, `h-10 px-4`. Outline is a 1px border on white. A navy variant appears on "Contact Sales": `bg-brand-navy` with white text ([site code](https://www.rud-co.com/assets/index-B04wKX3n.js)). Quote Desk uses blue fill for Approve and Send, outline for Edit and Export, and navy for nothing else.

## Honesty guardrails

- **Label it a concept.** Every screen and the video say it is a concept built for RudCo at Get Hired Hack. It is not a shipped RudCo product.
- **Label sample data.** The catalog, SKUs, prices and benchmarks are sample data unless RudCo gave them to us. The screen says so on every price, as the [PRD commits](PRD.md#commitments).
- **No endorsement.** Never say or imply that RudCo endorses, uses or sells Quote Desk.
- **No customers.** RudCo names no customer in public ([RudCo profile](../research/20261010.02_rudco_profile/rudco_profile.md#business-model-and-customers)). The sample buyer stays fictional (Northline Fabrication, per the [frontend contract](../engineering/FRONTEND_CONTRACT.md#processing-handoff)).
- **No stock claims.** Do not say an item is in stock in RudCo's real warehouse. A lead time of 24 to 48 hours appears only on lines the sample catalog marks as stocked, and it is labeled sample data. The site's product data shows a price of "0" and in-stock for every item, which reads as placeholder data ([RudCo profile](../research/20261010.02_rudco_profile/rudco_profile.md#business-model-and-customers)).
- **Flags, not certificates.** A compliance flag records what the buyer needs. It does not certify RudCo's material ([frontend contract](../engineering/FRONTEND_CONTRACT.md#model-and-view-interface)).
- **Quotes stay exact.** Any RudCo line used in the pitch is quoted word for word from its linked page.

## Decisions

- **The name is RudCo Quote Desk** (Ken, 2026-10-10). It names the rep's desk, where the agent prepares the quote and the rep has the conversation. It fits the founder's "People still want to deal with people." Ken's rule: this is an internal name for part of RudCo's service, not a standalone product, so other companies using the name does not matter.
