**Status:** Approved by Ken, 2026-10-10. The look lives in the [style guide](STYLE_GUIDE.md), also approved.

**Read with:** [the product brief](PRD.md), [the demo workflow](WORKFLOW.md), [the RudCo profile](../research/20261010.02_rudco_profile/rudco_profile.md), [the competitive analysis](../research/20261010.03_rudco_competitive_analysis/competitive_analysis.md), [the sharper-angle report](../research/20261010.01_rfq_sharper_angle/report_rfq_sharper_angle.md).

**Summary.** We present the product as a RudCo tool for its quoting reps. The name is RudCo Quote Desk. It looks like part of rud-co.com: RudCo's navy, its bright blue for actions, its system sans type, and its own logo files, unchanged. The message is one idea: every line on the quote shows where it came from. The pitch line is the brief's: the agent prepares the conversation, and the rep owns it. Every claim we make maps to a line RudCo has written itself ([alignment table](#how-this-aligns-with-rudcos-brand)). It is written as if it runs on RudCo's real catalog, inventory and prices.

## Claims we make, and claims we don't

**Price claims.** We never say RudCo is cheapest. Every price explanation says the price tracks published metal cost plus RudCo's margin ([WORKFLOW step 5](WORKFLOW.md#5-price-flag-compliance-list-assumptions)).

**We learn the buyer.** It is how we beat competitors on their own claim. Proton and Canals say they learn customers' shorthand. Ours shows where each learned reading came from: "Read as 0.060 in, as you confirmed on RFQ-0001" ([workflow step 9](WORKFLOW.md#9-learn-the-buyer), [competitive analysis](../research/20261010.03_rudco_competitive_analysis/competitive_analysis.md#where-rudco-can-get-ahead)).

**What we do not claim.** We do not imply credit terms, cutting, or ERP links. Lead times come from RudCo's inventory: stocked lines ship in 24 to 48 hours, and anything else says "to confirm". The demo covers quote preparation only ([competitive analysis](../research/20261010.03_rudco_competitive_analysis/competitive_analysis.md#what-our-demo-should-show)).

## Voice and messaging

### RudCo's voice, as it appears

- **Short, declarative lines.** "Industrial materials simplified." and "Precision. Transparency. Trust." ([site code](https://www.rud-co.com/assets/index-B04wKX3n.js)).
- **Names the cost of hiding things.** "Opacity is a tax on industrial procurement." and "The cost doesn’t show up as price. It shows up as friction." ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/www%2Erud-co%2Ecom_opacity-is-a-tax-on-industrial-procurement-activity-7451955371537059840-lsR0)).
- **Speaks to the buyer's doubt.** "The market moves every day. The quote in your inbox often doesn't." ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/www%2Erud-co%2Ecom_have-you-ever-wondered-if-youre-getting-activity-7459610821522833408--F-1)).
- **Keeps people central.** "People still want to deal with people, especially when the decision is important" ([Digital Journal](https://www.digitaljournal.com/article/ai-infrastructure-boom-reshapes-industrial-materials-procurement/)).
- **Plain buttons.** The site's most common button labels are "Request a Quote" and "Contact Us" ([site code](https://www.rud-co.com/assets/index-B04wKX3n.js)).

### Tagline options

Each sits under "Precision. Transparency. Trust."

1. **Every line shows its work.** (Recommended. It is the product in five words, and it answers "Opacity is a tax on industrial procurement.")
2. **Read right. Priced in the open. Sent by your rep.** (One phrase for each word of "Precision. Transparency. Trust.")
3. **A price with its date and its source.** (Answers "The market moves every day. The quote in your inbox often doesn't.")

**Pitch line** (from the [brief](PRD.md#who-it-is-for)): "The agent prepares the conversation, and the rep owns it." It restates the founder's "better information for the conversation" in our terms. Use it to close the video, as [WORKFLOW](WORKFLOW.md#the-video-demo-in-order) says.

### Words to use and avoid

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| quote, line, RFQ, buyer, rep | "AI-powered", "autonomous", "magic" |
| read as, matched to, priced against | "cheapest", "lowest price", "best price" |
| benchmark, dated, published metal cost plus RudCo's margin | "real-time" or "live" pricing (benchmarks are dated, not live) |
| compliance, compliance flag, mill test report (MTR), certificate of analysis (COA), country of origin, DFARS, REACH, RoHS | "certified" (we flag what the buyer needs; we do not certify stock) |
| lead time, in stock, ships in 24 to 48 hours (stocked lines), to confirm | |
| ask, question, needs the buyer, the conversation | "replaces your rep", "hands-free" |
| inventory, stocked | "instant", "guaranteed" |

### Sample UI microcopy

The SKU, benchmark and prices below are placeholders. The screen fills them from RudCo's catalog, inventory and benchmarks.

- **Ready line:** "Ready. Read as 304 stainless sheet, 0.0625 in (16 ga, stainless gauge table), 48 x 96 in. Matched to SKU [SKU]. Price tracks [benchmark name, date] plus RudCo's margin. Lead time: 24 to 48 hours (in stock)."
- **Lead time not known:** "Lead time: to confirm. This item is not in stock."
- **Unclear line, question not sent yet:** "Question to send. Line 3 says 16 gauge and 0.060 in. Those are different thicknesses. Question drafted."
- **Compliance flag:** "Mill test report needed. Triggered by "SA-240" in line 4, an ASME pressure-vessel spec."
- **COA flag:** "Certificate of analysis needed. Triggered by "COA required" in the email body. Applies to lines 2 and 3."
- **REACH / RoHS flag:** "REACH and RoHS declarations requested. Triggered by "REACH/RoHS compliant" in the attachment, page 1."
- **Context flag:** "DFARS origin rules may apply. Triggered by the ship-to address, not the line. Confirm with the buyer."
- **No match:** "No match. Nothing in RudCo's catalog fits this line. Not priced."
- **Buyer question email, subject:** "RFQ-0001, line 3: 16 gauge or 0.060 in?"
- **Buyer question email, opening line:** "Thanks for your RFQ. Lines 1 and 2 are priced. Before we quote line 3, one question: do you need 16 gauge (0.0625 in) or exactly 0.060 in? Click the one you need below."

## Honesty guardrails

- **No customers.** RudCo names no customer in public ([RudCo profile](../research/20261010.02_rudco_profile/rudco_profile.md#business-model-and-customers)). The sample buyer stays fictional (Northline Fabrication, per the [frontend contract](../engineering/FRONTEND_CONTRACT.md#processing-handoff)).
- **Flags, not certificates.** A compliance flag records what the buyer needs. It does not certify RudCo's material ([frontend contract](../engineering/FRONTEND_CONTRACT.md#model-and-view-interface)).
- **Quotes stay exact.** Any RudCo line used in the pitch is quoted word for word from its linked page.
