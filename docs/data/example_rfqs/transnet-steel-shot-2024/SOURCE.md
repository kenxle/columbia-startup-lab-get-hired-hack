## Item and quantity

The cover describes a twelve-month supply requirement on an as-needed basis. PDF page 6 identifies item 006828999 as S390 cast steel shot for abrasive sanding, hardness HRC 40-50, size designator 1.2 mm, packed in 1,000 kg boxes. Page 17 requests 50,000 kg in the price schedule. Dividing quantity by container size gives 50 boxes; this is a calculation, not a guaranteed order or delivery schedule.

## Usefulness and limits

Keep currency, freight, tax, and delivery assumptions explicit when comparing other sources.
