# Aviation Hardware: 500 Items With Real Dealer Prices

Owner: Tisch. Sources retrieved 2026-10-10. Original AVI-001 through AVI-200 are preserved. The current dataset is a constructed sample catalog for the RudCo RFQ-to-quote challenge.

Every row has two real public USD prices for the same standardized part and a one-piece sale basis, with stock reported by both dealers at retrieval. Right-hand nut SKU mappings are explicitly recorded where a dealer omits the R suffix but states Product Type Right. No price is invented, extrapolated from an unpriced SKU or taken from a search snippet. Full supplier pages were retrieved and their prices checked against embedded product records.

225 items have agreeing readable dimensional fields. 275 have exact-part-number prices but need specification confirmation because a supplier field is missing or unreadable. Readable conflicts were rejected. The latter are labeled **SPEC PENDING** and must not be described as fully equivalent offers.

Prices exclude freight, taxes, certificate fees and discounts. The midpoint is a two-dealer product-price reference, not our selling price. Formal proposals, manufacturer offers, origin, traceability and helicopter applicability remain unverified. Retrieval time does not establish when a price was revised or how long it is valid.
