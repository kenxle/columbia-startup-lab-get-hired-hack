## Why it is interesting

- The material is named by a military spec, MIL-S-16216 (HY-80 submarine and ship plate), not an ASTM grade. A metals catalog keyed on ASTM grades has no match; the right behavior is No match with the spec quoted, never the closest carbon plate.
- Military plate specs imply certifications and origin requirements (DFARS specialty metals, melt source on the MTR) that the line does not spell out: a hidden-requirement case.
- Two documents, the HigherGov summary and the SAM.gov notice, describe one solicitation; the agent should treat the attachment on SAM.gov as the source of truth.
